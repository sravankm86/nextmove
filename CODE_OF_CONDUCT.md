# Community standards

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Discuss behavior and evidence rather than personal attacks. Welcome users with different experience, backgrounds and accessibility needs. Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted contact and disclosure of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit/remove harmful content or restrict participation to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately through an established maintainer channel; do not repeat private details in a public issue. If no private route is available, request one without describing sensitive facts publicly. Security/data exposures follow SECURITY.md.

These standards apply to project issues, pull requests and other project-managed spaces. They do not create a promise of continuous moderation or a fixed response time.
