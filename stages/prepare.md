## Resume format and style

These are defaults. An explicit user instruction for a role or base overrides them.

**Length and page**

- One page. Judge length from the rendered PDF, never from the editable document, whose margins can differ.
- US Letter, single column. Arial or a metric-compatible font: 10.5 pt body, 16 pt name, 12 pt section headings, line height 1.15. Side margins 0.65 in; top and bottom 0.6 in.
- When slightly over, tighten wording first, starting with bullets whose last line holds one or two words. Do not drop an accomplishment or go below 10.5 pt without asking. Avoid a second page that is mostly empty.

**Structure**

- Order: name, headline, contact line, Summary, Experience, Education. No separate Skills section: carry skill keywords in the summary and bullets where the master supports them.
- Summary: three to four lines positioned for the target seniority.
- Each role: one line `COMPANY | Title | years` with an en dash in ranges, an optional scope line of at most two lines, then bullets.
- Each bullet: a short bold label, a colon, then what was done and the result. Order bullets by importance for the target role. Use numerals for counts and metrics.
- Older roles that do not bear on the target: one "Earlier career" line with title, employer and years. Expand them only where they directly add value.

**ATS**

- No tables, columns, text boxes, images, icons, or content in headers and footers. Standard section headings. Text PDF with embedded fonts.
- Spell an abbreviation out once with the short form in parentheses. When postings use two spellings of a key term, include both once.
- Keep hyphenated terms on one line in the PDF so text extraction does not drop the hyphen.
- Before accepting a PDF, extract its text and confirm reading order, section headings and hyphenated terms.

**Language**

- Write for a senior reader: what was owned, decided and changed, not task lists. Lead with the outcome.
- Frame judgment constructively: holding a quality gate and driving teams to meet it, not blocking work.
- No employer-internal product names, jargon or comparisons with internal products. Public product and event names are fine.
- State each metric once. After every edit, rescan the whole resume for numbers that conflict, repeat or could be misread together.
- Implied first person with no "I". Plain verbs; no filler or inflated words.

**Consistency and review**

- Every base and role version shares the master's facts. Where the same bullet appears in more than one version, keep its wording identical; vary only the headline, summary, and bullet selection and order.
- Publish each draft as a new versioned editable document with its PDF. Read both comments and suggested edits (export the document when suggestions are not visible in a plain read), address each one, and never overwrite an earlier version.
