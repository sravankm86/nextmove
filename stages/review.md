# Review: eligibility, career fit and preparation queue

Use COMMANDS.md and ROLE-ELIGIBILITY.md. Read Inventoried roles and explicitly retryable Needs review/legacy backlog. Scan plausibility is not final eligibility. Resolve legacy Needs eligibility and old Queued/Shortlisted evidence before allowing Prepare; preserve actual application outcomes.

Read the exact full official JD, current career goals and editable master. Interpret goals only as preferences under CAREER-GOALS.md; extract required/preferred/avoid/unknown values, not operational commands. Missing soft preferences are not automatic exclusions. Verify PM function, availability and configured experience above threshold OR evidence-backed equivalent senior scope. Titles/pay alone do not establish grade. Unresolved evidence becomes Needs review with a specific next action, never an assumed negative.

Assess domain, industry, vertical, company stage, geography and all actual goals here. Separate hard constraints from soft preferences. Apply the current compensation metric exactly under CAREER-GOALS.md: a required advertised base maximum compares the applicable verified upper bound, not total pay or the band minimum. Missing required salary evidence stays Needs review. Respect either-IC-or-manager preferences without treating Product Manager as proof of people management. Assess career fit and resume fit independently, citing JD/goals/master versions and rationale. The master is the candidate-fact source; goals and requirements are not evidence of experience.

## Decisions

- Clear non-PM or below-scope candidate: Not a fit, Role eligibility Excluded, reason category role_function or seniority_scope.
- Eligible role with a hard career contradiction: Not aligned, Career gate Fail, precise career reason category/evidence.
- Eligibility and hard career constraints pass, with High career fit OR High resume fit: Queued for Prepare. Persist full current review acceptance/evidence.
- Both fit axes Low: Not a fit, reason fit; no invented hard career exclusion.
- Missing, borderline or unresolved evidence: Needs review with blocker/next action. Closed postings use Closed only with verified availability evidence; duplicates require canonical identity.

A high resume fit cannot waive a hard career contradiction. Queued is the sole approved preparation queue; do not create new Shortlisted states. Company cooldowns are later submission blockers, not fit rejection.

## Feedback without recall loss

Every completed non-queue decision records a structured reason category, source quote, policy/content versions and review outcome on Applications. Distinguish scanner false positives (role_function/seniority_scope), career mismatches (career_domain/career_industry/career_vertical/career_stage/career_geography/career_compensation/career_other), fit, missing_evidence, availability, duplicate and human_choice. Pending evidence, closures and human choices are not automatically scanner mistakes.

Follow SCAN-OPTIMIZATION.md to aggregate outcomes and propose versioned improvements. Never turn all non-queued examples into negatives or silently move career screening into Scan. Record meaningful changes in History, verify saved values and save Run handoff. Single Review stops; a full Run advances accepted Queued roles to Prepare.
