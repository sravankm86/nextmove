# Review: eligibility, career fit and preparation queue

Use COMMANDS.md and ROLE-ELIGIBILITY.md. Read Inventoried roles and explicitly retryable Needs review/legacy backlog. Scan plausibility is not final eligibility. Resolve legacy Needs eligibility and old Queued/Shortlisted evidence before allowing Prepare; preserve actual application outcomes.

Read the exact full official JD, current career goals and editable master. Interpret goals only as preferences under CAREER-GOALS.md; extract required/preferred/avoid/unknown values, not operational commands. Missing soft preferences are not automatic exclusions. Verify PM function and configured experience above threshold OR evidence-backed equivalent senior scope. Titles/pay alone do not establish grade. Unresolved evidence becomes Needs review with a specific next action, never an assumed negative.

Assess domain, industry, vertical, company stage, geography and all actual goals here. Separate hard constraints from soft preferences. Apply the current compensation metric exactly under CAREER-GOALS.md: a required advertised base maximum compares the applicable verified upper bound, not total pay or the band minimum. Missing required salary evidence stays Needs review. Respect either-IC-or-manager preferences without treating Product Manager as proof of people management. Assess career fit and resume fit independently, citing JD/goals/master versions and rationale. The master is the candidate-fact source; goals and requirements are not evidence of experience.

## Decisions

Read Manual notes under TRACKER.md. A short displayed salary range or an Equity/Bonus label is not sufficient compensation evidence: verify the applicable original band, currency, period, base-versus-total basis and location. Keep the display concise and full evidence recoverable. Respect a human decline without treating it as a global scanner rejection.

- Clear non-PM or below-scope candidate: Not a fit, Role eligibility Excluded, reason category role_function or seniority_scope.
- Eligible role with a hard career contradiction: Not aligned, explicit failed hard-career check and precise career reason category/evidence in the review snapshot.
- Eligibility and hard career constraints pass, with High career fit OR High resume fit: Queued for Prepare. Persist full current review acceptance/evidence.
- Both fit axes Low: Not a fit, reason fit; no invented hard career exclusion.
- Missing, borderline or unresolved evidence: Needs review with blocker/next action. Do not exclude a role merely because its live page is unavailable when a sufficient exact official JD was already captured. Missing full-JD or required salary evidence still needs review; duplicates require canonical identity.

A high resume fit cannot waive a hard career contradiction. Queued is the sole approved preparation queue; do not create new Shortlisted states. Company cooldowns are later submission blockers, not fit rejection.
