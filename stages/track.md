# Track: submission evidence through Interviewing

Use WORKFLOW.md and DATA.md. Recruiting mail is read-only: do not send, draft, label, archive or delete. Missing account/scope access is a capability blocker, not completed reconciliation.

Process Applied roles and explicitly outstanding submission attempts. Interviewing is the endpoint: stop automatic tracking for that row once active recruiter engagement, screening, assessment, interview or scheduling is established. Do not create Offer/Accepted or later outcome states or continue polling completed interview-stage rows. Earlier employer rejection becomes Rejected; later facts supplied explicitly by the user may be preserved in History/company restriction evidence without extending this lifecycle.

Read the successful mailbox checkpoint from Settings/Run, overlap the prior window, paginate every relevant result and deduplicate stable message/event identities. Match exact requisition/job identity or unambiguous evidence; company-only acknowledgements remain unresolved. A bounded access probe is not reconciliation and must not advance the mailbox checkpoint.

Definite submission or an exact-role receipt establishes Applied. Confirmation adds a compact Confirmation reference and exact receipt/message evidence in History without a separate Confirmed status. Active recruiter engagement → Interviewing. Explicit pre-interview employer rejection → Rejected. Preserve actual occurrence time separately from recording time; an older acknowledgement cannot regress later stronger history. Contradictions need correction evidence.

Uncertain submissions are reconciled, never resubmitted automatically. Missing mail does not establish non-submission. Keep the saved attempt blocking until the actual outcome is resolved, regardless of manual status edits.

Record message/receipt identities, confirmation/rejection/engagement dates and evidence in History. Update current status/Confirmation reference and concise Agent notes; preserve human-owned Manual notes and earlier useful evidence. Runs/Settings retain exact mailbox scope, cursor, counts and unresolved identities; no separate mail-audit table is needed.

A rejection changes a company hold only under a confirmed applicable rule; preserve exact trigger scope/time and never invent a company-wide ban. Verify all saved changes and handoff. A single Track stops; a full Run reports remaining required gaps.

For a first Track without a successful checkpoint, use the earliest known application/observation event among tracked scope, record the basis and paginate from it. Observation timestamps come from History, not a removed Last seen at column. If the boundary cannot be established, ask under WORKFLOW.md while continuing independent work.
