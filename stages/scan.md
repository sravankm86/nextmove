# Scan: plausible PM inventory with recall first

Follow COMMANDS.md and ROLE-ELIGIBILITY.md. Read explicit Allow/Exclude choices, every configured official board/region and the versioned scan/role policy. Resolve the current Career Goals source revision and extract/verify its role-family, designation examples, desired seniority/experience/scope and explicit function/level exclusions under CAREER-GOALS.md. These determine broad scan criteria; Scan does not assess broader career fit or resume fit and cannot set Queued. Role-policy values are source-versioned extracted preferences, not an independent preference authority or user-authored procedures. At startup verify the source revision, refresh affected role-policy values and save criteria/readiness plus unresolved inputs before applying a screen. A current verified extraction may be reused; stale or untraceable Settings values may not silently override the document.

## Complete source coverage

Enumerate every official catalog page/cursor without storing unrelated rows to verified end. Validate counts, unique identities, repeated cursors, malformed/empty pages, caps and source-manifest gaps. Zero requires verified empty coverage. Search snippets or unchanged page-one counts do not establish completeness. Reuse unchanged complete resources/change feeds only with validated equivalent evidence and durable checkpoints.

Use repeatable official feeds/readers and code for parsing, identities, broad title screening, hashes and verification of saved values. Preserve meaningful URL parameters and genuine source memberships. Title equality is not deduplication.

## Basic candidate screen

Use broad PM keywords, aliases, localized and unlevelled titles. Check official title, team/function, summary or readily available description for plausible product-management work and senior scope. Exclude clear unrelated functions and clearly junior roles. Do not infer company grade from title, demand Senior in every title, or treat low minimum years alone as proof of insufficient scope.

Basic semantics must have recorded evidence or an explicit ambiguity reason; a keyword hit is not final eligibility. Plausible candidates with uncertain PM boundaries or level enter Inventoried with Role eligibility Pending for Review. Do not perform full career/domain/industry/vertical/geography alignment, resume scoring or exhaustive seniority adjudication here. Capture geographic/work restrictions as data, not career-fit exclusions. Explicit company Exclude remains binding.

If identity or official availability cannot be established, keep a minimal Run pending check with URL, reason and next action rather than inventing a role. Missing title can be resolved from official summary; insufficient evidence for even plausibility stays pending, not silently rejected.

## Inventory and handoff

Follow TRACKER.md for compact display fields: salary ranges only, explicit additional-compensation labels, short place values and the permitted work-arrangement values. Keep location-specific salary conditions and exact source text in evidence; no raw JSON or explanatory paragraphs in display columns. Preserve Manual notes and any manual disposition on reobservation.

Create or update candidates as Inventoried, recording stable identity/source links, official URL and availability plus a verified Scan run and basic-semantic metadata snapshot under STAGE-STATE.md. Save Job evidence and an exact indexed source capture containing full description/provenance, employer posting aliases, salary basis/currency/location/level, work restrictions and true posting date. Keep compact display values on Applications; save explicit gaps in Blocker/Run. Never estimate metadata or borrow another posting's band. Missing full description does not discard an otherwise plausible candidate; Review must obtain it before approval.

Career gate starts Pending; Scan does not certify final Role eligibility. Reobserving a role updates only permitted source/metadata deltas; preserve human outcomes, existing review evidence, notes and later states. Changed evidence flags targeted re-review without reopening submitted/terminal roles. Unchanged rejected roles are not requeued every scan.

Clear rejects produce aggregate reason counts, not a raw shadow catalog. Capture candidate descriptions in registered Drive content; unrelated feed bodies remain transient. Sources holds cursor/coverage; Runs holds scope, counts, gaps and handoff; Applications holds candidate/review/application state.

Verify writes and all planned sources. Company/source Complete requires every planned source verified; Partial means usable observations with coverage gaps; Failed means no usable verified source. Report source coverage separately from capture gaps and unresolved candidates. Review pending does not by itself make completed Scan coverage incomplete. A full Run remains Partial if required later work is blocked.

Report observed, clear-rejected, plausible, newly inventoried, existing/reobserved, identity-pending and capture-gap counts separately. A single Scan stops; a full Run hands Inventoried roles to Review.
