# Fictional contract examples

Candidate preferences: product leadership, enterprise AI, US remote. Fictional master evidence: owned rollout of a document-review assistant and evaluated outputs against human reviewers. It does not establish healthcare experience.

## Worker queues and fit decisions

- An Allow company with two official boards has one verified catalog and one timeout. Scanner records Partial, keeps all observed inventory and prior observations, and queues verified broad matches without claiming complete coverage.
- A verified empty catalog is Complete/zero; an inaccessible catalog is Failed. Missing salary or posted date stays unknown, not estimated or replaced with date found.
- Career fit High + resume fit Low → Shortlisted with the resume gap disclosed. Career fit Low + resume fit High also → Shortlisted. The rule is OR.
- Both Low → Not a fit. Medium/Medium or Low/Unresolved → Needs review owned by Reviewer, not a fabricated negative. An unavailable full JD is retryable.
- Changed relevant JD/criteria/master evidence flags review_pending for a pre-submission Not a fit record; unchanged negatives are not reviewed repeatedly.
- An old Reviewed/Rated row is not blindly renamed Shortlisted. Submitted or terminal history is not reopened just because fit changed.

## Resume integrity and application gates

- A suitable approved base PDF can become Ready after factual and visual verification, with “base reused; no tailoring” notes and master/version grounding.
- A JD asks for healthcare experience absent from the master. Preparer records that gap and never adds healthcare claims, metrics or tenure. Unsupported draft wording blocks Ready.
- A company hold leaves the Allow company in the scan and preserves fit/status; Applier rechecks the hold, current quota and live controls before Submit.
- Human reports submission → Applied; exact-role receipt → Confirmed. A possible click followed by a crash → Submission uncertain and Tracker reconciliation, not a blind retry.

## Tracker matching and chronology

- Two roles exist at a company. “We received your application” without role identity stays ambiguous, not attributed to both.
- An exact-role interview invitation supports In process. A receipt alone or silence does not.
- An older acknowledgement arriving after a rejection does not overwrite Rejected. A questionnaire shortly after a rejection is not an assumed reversal without explicit correction.
- Repeated polling deduplicates the same message/event and never creates another submission or quota event. A rejection does not invent a company-wide cooldown.
- Without mailbox access or complete pagination, Tracker reports a coverage/checkpoint gap; it does not claim reconciliation. Mail remains read-only.
